> starting point that covers what UK GDPR Article 28 requires of a processor.

> solicitor and fill in every **[SQUARE-BRACKETED]** field. Re-use the same

## Background

A. The Customer is a recruitment agency that determines the purposes and means
   of processing personal data about candidates, client contacts and other
   individuals (the **Controller**).

B. The Customer uses the Altus recruitment CRM (the **"Service"**) to store and
   process that personal data. In doing so, Altus acts as a **Processor** on the
   Customer's behalf.

C. This DPA governs that processing and forms part of, and is subject to, the
   agreement under which the Customer uses the Service (the **"Principal
   Agreement"**). Where this DPA conflicts with the Principal Agreement on data
   protection, this DPA prevails.

---

## 1. Definitions

1.1 Terms such as **"personal data"**, **"processing"**, **"controller"**,
   **"processor"**, **"data subject"**, **"personal data breach"** and
   **"supervisory authority"** have the meanings given in **UK GDPR** and the
   **Data Protection Act 2018** (together, **"Data Protection Law"**).

1.2 **"UK GDPR"** means the retained EU law version of Regulation (EU) 2016/679
   as it forms part of the law of England and Wales, Scotland and Northern
   Ireland.

1.3 **"Sub-processor"** means any third party engaged by Altus to process
   personal data on the Customer's behalf (listed in **Annex 3**).

1.4 **"Customer Personal Data"** means personal data that Altus processes on the
   Customer's behalf under the Service, as described in **Annex 1**.

---

## 2. Roles and scope

2.1 The Customer is the **Controller** and Altus is the **Processor** in respect
   of the Customer Personal Data.

2.2 Each party will comply with its obligations under Data Protection Law.

2.3 Altus will process Customer Personal Data only as set out in this DPA, the
   Principal Agreement, and the Customer's documented instructions — including
   the instructions inherent in the Customer's configuration and ordinary use of
   the Service. Annex 1 sets out the agreed details of processing.

2.4 The Customer warrants that it has a lawful basis (and, where required, has
   obtained the necessary consents and provided the necessary privacy
   information) for Altus to process the Customer Personal Data as instructed.

---

## 3. Altus's obligations as Processor (UK GDPR Art. 28(3))

Altus will:

3.1 **(a) Documented instructions.** Process Customer Personal Data only on the
   Customer's documented instructions (including regarding international
   transfers), unless required to do otherwise by law — in which case Altus will
   inform the Customer first, unless that law prohibits it. Altus will tell the
   Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Law.

3.2 **(b) Confidentiality.** Ensure that persons authorised to process the
   Customer Personal Data are bound by an appropriate duty of confidentiality.

3.3 **(c) Security.** Implement and maintain appropriate technical and
   organisational measures to protect the Customer Personal Data, taking account
   of the state of the art, costs, and the risks to data subjects (UK GDPR Art.
   32). The current measures are described in **Annex 2**.

3.4 **(d) Sub-processors.** Not engage another processor without the Customer's
   prior authorisation. The Customer gives **general written authorisation** for
   the sub-processors listed in **Annex 3**. Altus will give the Customer at
   least **[30] days'** prior notice of any intended addition or replacement of a
   sub-processor (e.g. by updating Annex 3 and notifying the Customer), giving
   the Customer the opportunity to object on reasonable data-protection grounds.
   Altus will impose data-protection obligations on each sub-processor that are
   no less protective than those in this DPA, and remains liable to the Customer
   for each sub-processor's performance.

3.5 **(e) Data-subject rights.** Taking account of the nature of the processing,
   assist the Customer by appropriate technical and organisational measures, so
   far as possible, to respond to data subjects exercising their rights (access,
   rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection). The Service
   provides self-service tooling for export and erasure; for anything those
   tools do not cover, Altus will provide reasonable assistance.

3.6 **(f) Assistance with compliance.** Assist the Customer in ensuring
   compliance with its obligations under UK GDPR Art. 32–36 (security, breach
   notification, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation),
   taking into account the nature of processing and the information available to
   Altus.

3.7 **(g) Deletion or return.** At the Customer's choice, delete or return all
   Customer Personal Data at the end of the provision of the Service, and delete
   existing copies, unless retention is required by law. See clause 7.

3.8 **(h) Audits.** Make available to the Customer all information reasonably
   necessary to demonstrate compliance with this clause 3, and allow for and
   contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by the Customer or
   another auditor it mandates — on reasonable prior notice, no more than once a
   year (unless required by a supervisory authority or following a breach),
   subject to confidentiality and minimising disruption.

---

## 4. Personal data breaches

4.1 Altus will notify the Customer **without undue delay** after becoming aware
   of a personal data breach affecting the Customer Personal Data, and in any
   event within **[72] hours** of becoming aware.

4.2 The notification will describe, to the extent known: the nature of the
   breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records
   affected, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed.

4.3 Altus will provide reasonable assistance to the Customer in meeting the
   Customer's own breach-notification obligations to the ICO and to affected
   data subjects. Altus will not notify the ICO or data subjects on the
   Customer's behalf unless the Customer instructs it to.

---

## 5. International transfers

5.1 Altus will not transfer Customer Personal Data outside the UK except as
   described in **Annex 3** (sub-processor locations) or on the Customer's
   instructions.

5.2 Where Customer Personal Data is transferred to a country without UK adequacy
   regulations, the transfer will be protected by an appropriate safeguard under
   UK GDPR Art. 46 (e.g. the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA), or
   the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses), and supplementary
   measures where needed.

---

## 6. Security measures

6.1 Altus's technical and organisational measures are set out in **Annex 2**.
   Altus may update them provided the level of protection is not reduced.

---

## 7. Duration, deletion and return

7.1 This DPA takes effect on the effective date and continues for as long as
   Altus processes Customer Personal Data under the Principal Agreement.

7.2 On termination or expiry of the Service, or on the Customer's earlier
   request, Altus will — at the Customer's choice — return the Customer Personal
   Data (via the Service's export feature) and/or delete it, including from
   live systems and backups (backups are overwritten on their normal rotation
   cycle of **[30] days**), unless retention is required by law.

7.3 The Service provides the Customer with self-service export and erasure tools
   it can use at any time during the term.

---

## 8. Liability and general

8.1 Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations and
   exclusions of liability in the Principal Agreement.

8.2 This DPA is governed by the laws of **England and Wales**, and the parties
   submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.

8.3 If any provision is held invalid, the remainder continues in force.

## Annex 1 — Details of the processing

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Subject matter** | Provision of the Altus recruitment CRM to the Customer. |
| **Duration** | The term of the Principal Agreement (see clause 7). |
| **Nature & purpose** | Hosting, storage, organisation, retrieval, AI-assisted parsing/matching/search, and communication tooling, to enable the Customer to manage candidates and recruitment activity. |
| **Types of personal data** | Identity & contact details (name, email, phone, location); CV content (work history, skills, education, salary information); application and candidacy data; recruiter notes and correspondence; technical data (e.g. hashed IP for the public application form). |
| **Special category data** | Not intentionally processed. The Customer must not enter special-category data (e.g. health, ethnicity) into free-text fields unless it has a valid Art. 9 condition and has configured the Service accordingly. |

| **Processing operations** | Collection, recording, storage, retrieval, AI analysis (parsing, embedding, match-scoring, transcription), disclosure to the Customer's authorised users, and deletion. |

## Annex 2 — Technical and organisational measures (Art. 32)

- **Tenant isolation:** every data record is scoped to the Customer's
  organisation and protected by database Row-Level Security, so one customer's
  data is not accessible to another.
- **Access control:** access requires authentication (passwordless magic-link
  sign-in); application access is role-scoped; privileged/administrative access
  is restricted to authorised Altus personnel.
- **Encryption:** data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest by the
  underlying hosting/database provider.
- **Secrets management:** API keys and credentials are stored as protected
  environment secrets, not in source code.
- **AI cost & abuse controls:** per-tenant usage accounting and spend caps.
- **Audit logging:** access to candidate detail and key actions are logged with
  organisation and actor context; logs exclude raw candidate PII.
- **Monitoring:** application errors are monitored (with PII excluded from error
  reports).
- **Backups:** the database provider maintains automated backups; data is
  removed from backups on their normal rotation cycle after deletion.
- **Sub-processor controls:** sub-processors are bound by equivalent
  data-protection terms (Annex 3).
- **Data minimisation in logs:** CV text, candidate names and emails are never
  written to error-monitoring or analytics tooling.
