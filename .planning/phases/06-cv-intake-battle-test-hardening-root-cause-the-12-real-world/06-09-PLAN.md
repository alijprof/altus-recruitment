Run the mandatory pre-UAT pipeline before the founder is asked to touch anything.

The project's hard rule is explicit and non-optional: `/gsd-code-review` PLUS an
**AI-driven browser-automation pre-smoke** must both run, and their findings must
be fixed, before any human is asked to test — especially for a change involving

bugs; the mechanical review plus an automated browser walkthrough catches 7–10 of
them. The human should only ever be verifying the residual 1–2 subjective items.

upload paths, the failure-message surface and the retry affordance. That is a
wide blast radius across a LIVE product with real customer data. It gets reviewed
and driven in a real browser before it is offered for sign-off.

dispositions and both walkthroughs.
