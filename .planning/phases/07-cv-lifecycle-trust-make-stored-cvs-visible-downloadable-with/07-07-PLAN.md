<objective>
Remove the refresh-to-see friction from match scoring and add a one-click bulk
score on the matches page.

Purpose: The Explain action already calls `revalidatePath`, but the client is
never told to re-fetch — so the toast literally instructs the recruiter to
refresh the page (explain-button.tsx:36). And scoring ten candidates today
means ten separate clicks and ten separate waits. Implements D-04 (freshness
half).

Output: Self-refreshing Explain, plus a `Score all` control wired to the
existing precompute pipeline.
</objective>
